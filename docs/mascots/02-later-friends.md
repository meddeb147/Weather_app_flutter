## 6. Seal Pup: "The Snack Lover"

**Personality:** playful foodie, big cravings, chocolate everywhere, round belly.
Food is joy, never guilt.

**Design**

| Element | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Silhouette key | A lying-down teardrop: no ears, no neck, two front flippers and a little split tail |
| Body | The roundest belly in the family; she rests on it and rolls |
| Face | Extra-large dark eyes, three tiny whisker dots per cheek, small smile often with a chocolate smudge |
| Flippers | Short and rounded; she holds snacks against her belly with both |
| Props | Heart-shaped chocolate box, cookie, strawberry mochi, a crumb trail |
| Moon charm | On a small round bib collar |

**Palette:** main marshmallow white `#F7F4F0`, secondary warm grey shading
`#D9D2CC`, accent strawberry pink `#FF8FA3`, chocolate `#7A4B3A`.

**Phase poses**

| Phase | Pose |
| --- | --- |
| Menstrual | On her back, chocolate box balanced on her belly, one square mid-bite, blissful |
| Follicular | Balancing a strawberry on her nose, flippers out, playful |
| Ovulation | Clapping flippers, sparkling eyes, a perfect little cake in front of her |
| Luteal (PMS) | Hugging a large cookie protectively, cheeks full, crumbs around her, daring you to judge (you don't) |

**Reactions**

- Gift: sniffs, eyes widen, claps, eats or hugs it at once.
- Happy: belly roll.
- Sad: flat on her belly, empty wrapper beside her.
- Sleepy: food coma, flipper on belly.
- Celebrating: tosses a snack in the air and catches it.

**Best-friend stage detail:** a tiny strawberry print on her bib.

## 7. Tiger Cub: "The Tiny Warrior"

**Personality:** brave and busy, still goes to work and the gym, hates slowing
down. Tiny body, huge determination.

**Design**

| Element | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Silhouette key | Round ears set wide, a short thick tail held straight up, and a wide planted stance |
| Body | Sturdy bean, chest slightly forward |
| Face | Determined eyes with a small upward inner-corner tilt; cream muzzle; tiny confident smirk |
| Markings | Soft, rounded cocoa stripes: three on the forehead, two on each cheek, three on the tail. Stripes are thick and few, like felt pieces. |
| Props | Sweatband, tiny dumbbell, water bottle, laptop, small bandage for brave days |
| Moon charm | Centre of her sweatband |

**Palette:** main apricot `#FFB870` (clearly more yellow and paler than Momo's
red-orange), secondary cream `#FFF4E1`, stripes cocoa `#7D5240`, accent teal
`#5CC8C0` (sweatband).

**Phase poses**

| Phase | Pose |
| --- | --- |
| Menstrual | Standing with a hot water bottle strapped on like a belt, fist raised, slightly wobbly but smiling |
| Follicular | Mid-jog, sweatband on, tail up, bright grin |
| Ovulation | Victory pose on a small podium block, both arms up |
| Luteal (PMS) | Sitting down reluctantly with a water bottle, towel over shoulders, small frown: learning to rest |

**Reactions**

- Gift: salutes, then hugs it when she thinks nobody is looking.
- Happy: fist pump.
- Sad: sits, tail drops, sweatband slips over one eye.
- Sleepy: falls asleep standing, dumbbell still in paw.
- Celebrating: flexes tiny arms, confetti cannon.

**Tone note:** her tips are "train smart on your period", so her period pose
shows adapting, not suffering through it.

**Best-friend stage detail:** a small star stitched on her sweatband.

## 8. Penguin: "The Little Planner"

**Personality:** organized, always prepared, knows her cycle day, loves charts.
Carries the supplies everyone else forgot.

**Design**

| Element | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Silhouette key | Upright egg with a small rounded beak and two flipper arms held like she is carrying a clipboard |
| Body | Tall egg, the most upright posture in the family |
| Face | Round eyes behind large, thin round glasses (her signature); tiny peach beak |
| Markings | Navy-plum back and hood, white face and belly in one smooth heart-topped shape |
| Props | Clipboard with a checklist of tick shapes (no text), tiny calendar, pencil behind the head, neat pouch of supplies |
| Moon charm | Pin on a short knitted scarf |

**Palette:** main navy-plum `#5B5F97`, secondary white `#FFFFFF`, accent peach
`#FFB89A` (beak, feet), scarf mint `#B8E6D0`.

**Phase poses**

| Phase | Pose |
| --- | --- |
| Menstrual | Sitting with her prepared kit laid out in a neat row (tea, heat pad, snack), ticking the last box, content |
| Follicular | Pointing at a small rising chart, glasses gleaming |
| Ovulation | Holding the calendar up proudly with today circled, sparkle on the glasses |
| Luteal (PMS) | Glasses slightly crooked, checklist longer than her, one flipper on her forehead, still coping |

**Reactions**

- Gift: checks her list, discovers it was not on the list, delighted flap.
- Happy: adjusts glasses, small nod, tick mark pops up.
- Sad: glasses fog, clipboard lowered.
- Sleepy: asleep on her clipboard, pencil still behind her head.
- Celebrating: throws the checklist pages as confetti, then tidies one up.

**Best-friend stage detail:** a small bookmark ribbon on her clipboard.

## 9. Capybara: "The Unbothered Queen"

**Personality:** chill, irregular cycle, never stresses, goes with the flow.
Her calm is real (unlike Duck's).

**Design**

| Element | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Silhouette key | A soft rounded-rectangle loaf with a blunt square muzzle and tiny ears; the only boxy shape in the family |
| Body | Low, wide, heavy; sits like a loaf or soaks up to her chin |
| Face | Permanently half-closed, peaceful eyes; flat little smile; very small ears set far back |
| Limbs | Almost hidden under the body |
| Props | Round wooden hot-spring tub with steam shapes, a small towel folded on her head, a floating citrus fruit |
| Moon charm | Hangs from the leaf of a small flower resting on her head |

**Palette:** main warm caramel `#D2A679`, secondary light oat `#EBD3B0`
(muzzle, belly), accent leaf green `#9CCB8B`, flower soft yellow `#FFE48F`.

**Phase poses**

| Phase | Pose |
| --- | --- |
| Menstrual | Soaking in the tub up to her chin, towel on head, steam rising, perfectly at peace |
| Follicular | Sitting in grass, a small butterfly on her nose, unbothered |
| Ovulation | Standing (rare), flower in full bloom, a slow contented smile |
| Luteal (PMS) | Same loaf, same face, with one tiny storm cloud above her that she ignores |

**Irregular-cycle note:** her charm has an extra "soft glow, phase uncertain"
state (a hazy moon behind a small cloud) for when the app shows a date range
instead of a single date.

**Reactions**

- Gift: slow turn of the head, slow smile, places it on her head.
- Happy: ears wiggle. That is all, and it is enough.
- Sad: sinks a little lower in the water, one bubble.
- Sleepy: identical to neutral, eyes fully closed.
- Celebrating: other small friends (butterfly, bird) celebrate on top of her while she smiles.

**Best-friend stage detail:** a second flower and a tiny bird friend.

## 10. Axolotl: "The Self-Care Princess"

**Personality:** always smiling, loves face masks, bubble baths, candles,
treating herself. Self-care is her whole philosophy.

**Design**

| Element | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Silhouette key | Wide flat head with three feathery gill stalks on each side, like a crown |
| Body | Small, slim bean with a soft paddle tail; the head is extra wide |
| Face | Eyes set very far apart, permanent wide gentle smile (the widest mouth in the family) |
| Gills | Simplified into three soft rounded fronds per side; they are an animation channel: perk up (happy), droop (tired), flutter (excited) |
| Presentation | Like Pufferfish, she floats in a soft bubble or sits in her bubble bath |
| Props | Sheet face mask, cucumber slices, bubble bath tub, candle, tiny towel turban, bath bomb |
| Moon charm | Clip on her top-left gill frond |

**Palette:** main blush pink `#FFC4D6`, secondary pale cream-pink `#FFEAF0`,
gills raspberry `#F2709C`, accent lilac `#CDB4F0`.

**Phase poses**

| Phase | Pose |
| --- | --- |
| Menstrual | In a bubble bath with a towel turban, candle beside her, eyes closed, gills relaxed |
| Follicular | Fresh-faced, patting her cheeks, gills perked, sparkle on skin |
| Ovulation | Posing with a hand mirror, full glow, gills fluttering |
| Luteal (PMS) | Sheet mask on, cucumber slices over her eyes, lying back, still smiling under the mask |

**Reactions**

- Gift: clasps hands, gills flutter, adds it to her self-care tray.
- Happy: little shimmy, bubbles rise.
- Sad: gills droop, smile smaller but still there; lights a candle.
- Sleepy: silk sleep mask on, floats gently.
- Celebrating: bath-bomb fizz burst in pastel colours.

**Best-friend stage detail:** a tiny tiara of bubbles.

## Family line-up check

When all 10 stand together, confirm:

- Momo is the only red-orange character and stands at the centre front.
- No two mascots share a main colour: red-orange, cream-white, butter yellow,
  dove grey, aqua mint, marshmallow white, apricot, navy-plum, caramel, blush pink.
  (Cat and Seal Pup are both pale: Cat is separated by her lilac cap and ears,
  Seal Pup by her earless teardrop shape.)
- Heights are within 15% of each other; Capybara is the widest, Penguin the tallest.
- Each silhouette passes the 48 px test.
- Every moon charm is visible from the front.
