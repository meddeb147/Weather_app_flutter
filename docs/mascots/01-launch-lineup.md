# Character Sheets: Launch Lineup (V1)

Momo, Cat, Duck, Koala, Pufferfish. Read `00-style-guide.md` first; shared rules
are not repeated here.

---

## 1. Momo the Red Panda: "The Emotional Softie"

Main mascot, app icon, leader of the crew.

**Personality:** big heart, feels everything, cries at movies, needs hugs and
reassurance. She is the one who notices when someone else is sad.

**Design**

| Element | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Silhouette key | Round head with two small rounded teardrop ears, and a short fat tail curled to one side like a comma |
| Body | Soft egg shape, slightly pear-bottomed, with a cream **heart-shaped belly patch** (her signature) |
| Face | Cream mask covering the lower two thirds of the face in one smooth rounded shape; two tiny cream dots above the eyes |
| Ears | Small, rounded, peach inside. No tufts. |
| Tail | Short and plump, about the size of her head, with exactly two soft cream bands |
| Paws | Chocolate-brown mittens on all four limbs |
| Moon charm | Pendant on a thin cream cord necklace, resting at the top of the heart patch |

**Palette:** main `#F26B4E` (brand red-orange), secondary cream `#FFF1E0`,
accent chocolate `#6B3A2E`, inner ear peach `#FFC9A8`.

**Originality guardrails (must not resemble Mei from *Turning Red*)**

- Tiny plush scale, never large or towering. No fluffy mane, neck ruff or cheek fur spikes.
- No white ear tufts, no thick eyebrow patches, no long ringed tail.
- No human-teen cues: no hair, glasses, beanie, clothes from that film's wardrobe, no "poof" transformation smoke.
- Smooth clay surface, not shaggy fur. Her identifying marks are the heart belly and the comma tail.

**Phase poses**

| Phase | Pose |
| --- | --- |
| Menstrual | Sitting, hugging her own tail like a pillow, eyes glossy, one big tear wobbling but not falling. Small hot water bottle on her lap. |
| Follicular | Standing on tiptoe, arms slightly out, head tilted, curious smile |
| Ovulation | Arms wide open offering a hug, heart patch faintly glowing, strongest blush |
| Luteal (PMS) | Sitting with a tissue box, lower lip wobbling, ears drooped, holding a tiny chocolate square |

**Reactions**

- Gift: gasps, eyes become huge, hugs the gift to her heart patch, happy tear.
- Happy: two small hops, tail wags.
- Sad: ears down, hugs tail, single tear.
- Sleepy: curls into a ball with tail over her nose.
- Celebrating: throws heart-shaped confetti, spins once.

**Best-friend stage detail:** a tiny cream heart on the tip of her tail.

## 2. Cat: "The Moody Diva"

**Personality:** independent and dramatic. "Don't touch me"… five minutes
later: "Why is nobody petting me?" Tail and ears do all the talking.

**Design**

| Element | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Silhouette key | Sharp-ish triangular ears (rounded tips) and a long expressive tail, the longest in the family |
| Body | Compact bean. Folds into a perfect bread "loaf" |
| Face | Slightly smaller, half-lidded eyes by default (the only mascot with a default side-eye). Tiny "3" mouth. |
| Markings | Cream-white body with a lilac-grey cap over the ears and a lilac-grey tail tip |
| Ears | Animation channel: up (interested), sideways "airplane" (annoyed), flat (leave me alone) |
| Tail | Animation channel: question-mark curl (happy), slow flick (irritated), wrapped around body (needs comfort) |
| Moon charm | Clip on her left ear |

**Palette:** main cream-white `#FBF4EC`, secondary lilac-grey `#B9A9D6`,
accent rose `#F28CA5`, inner ear pink `#FFD3DC`.

**Phase poses**

| Phase | Pose |
| --- | --- |
| Menstrual | Full loaf inside a rolled blanket, only face and ear tips visible, one eye open |
| Follicular | Sitting tall, chin up, tail in a question-mark curl, small proud smile |
| Ovulation | Mid-strut, eyes fully open and sparkling, tail high. Her one fully wide-eyed pose. |
| Luteal (PMS) | Back half-turned to camera, looking over her shoulder, airplane ears, tail flicking. Clearly wants attention. |

**Reactions**

- Gift: pretends not to care, glances, then pulls it close with one paw and purrs (blush up).
- Happy: slow blink, tail curl.
- Sad: loaf, ears flat, tail wrapped tight.
- Sleepy: big yawn, then melts sideways.
- Celebrating: one elegant spin, lands in a pose, pretends it was nothing.

**Best-friend stage detail:** a small rose bow at the base of her tail.

## 3. Duck: "Calm Outside, Chaos Inside"

**Personality:** looks peaceful, acts fine at work, hides her cramps, then
collapses at home. The joke is always the contrast, never her.

**Design**

| Element | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Silhouette key | Smooth round head, flat wide bill, and one small curled feather tuft on top |
| Body | Teardrop sitting low, like a rubber duck but softer and wider |
| Face | Serene default smile: eyes slightly closed in a calm arc. Bill is short, flat and rounded. |
| Wings | Small rounded flaps held neatly against the body |
| Feet | Tiny orange paddles; they are an animation channel (see below) |
| Water prop | A shallow round "puddle" disc she can sit in, with a transparent waterline layer |
| Moon charm | Centre of a small daisy tucked beside her head tuft |

**Palette:** main butter yellow `#FFE08A`, secondary soft orange `#FFA860`
(bill, feet), accent pond blue `#A9DDF0`, daisy white `#FFFDF5`.

**Signature animation:** split view. Above the waterline: calm face, gentle
bob. Below the waterline: little legs paddling frantically. The underwater
layer is separate so the app can show or hide it.

**Phase poses**

| Phase | Pose |
| --- | --- |
| Menstrual | Floating with the calm smile, wing holding a tiny heat patch on her belly; underwater feet paddling fast |
| Follicular | Standing on land, wings slightly lifted, real open smile, a relaxed stretch |
| Ovulation | Gliding smoothly across the puddle, ripples sparkling, feet calm for once |
| Luteal (PMS) | Calm face with one small sweat drop, holding a to-do list; feet at maximum speed |

**Reactions**

- Gift: calm "oh, thank you", then a delighted wing-flap she fails to hold in.
- Happy: tail wiggle, small quack bubble (a shape, no text).
- Sad: calm face slips, bill trembles, sinks lower in the water.
- Sleepy: tucks bill under wing, floats in a slow circle.
- Celebrating: big splash, daisy petals as confetti.

**Private "at home" pose (used on her own screen, not shared by default):**
flat on her back like a pancake, wings out, finally resting.

**Best-friend stage detail:** a second tiny daisy.

## 4. Koala: "The Sleepy Cuddler"

**Personality:** exhausted, wants bed, blankets, heating pad, cancels plans
without guilt. The softest and slowest of the family.

**Design**

| Element | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Silhouette key | Two very large fluffy round ears, the widest silhouette in the family |
| Body | Heavy-bottomed pear, always slightly slumped |
| Face | Sleepy droopy eyelids by default. Nose is a small soft oval (larger than other mascots, still simplified). |
| Ears | Big, round, with pale pink plush inside |
| Limbs | Short arms made for hugging; she is almost always holding something |
| Props | Small leaf-print pillow, moon-pattern blanket, heating pad |
| Moon charm | On the drooping tip of a soft nightcap |

**Palette:** main dove grey `#C9CED8`, secondary white `#FAFAFC`, accent
eucalyptus green `#A8D5BA`, nightcap dusty blue `#B5C7E8`, nose `#6E6878`.

**Phase poses**

| Phase | Pose |
| --- | --- |
| Menstrual | Lying on her side under the blanket hugging a heating pad, nightcap on, eyes closed, tiny smile |
| Follicular | Sitting up, mid-stretch, nightcap pushed back, eyes actually open |
| Ovulation | Standing, hugging her pillow, the most awake she ever gets, soft sparkle |
| Luteal (PMS) | Wrapped in the blanket like a burrito, only face and ears out, heavy eyelids, phone face-down beside her (plans cancelled) |

**Reactions**

- Gift: slow blink, slow smile, hugs it and falls asleep on it.
- Happy: gentle sway side to side.
- Sad: pulls nightcap over her eyes.
- Sleepy: her richest animation: yawn, nod, tip over onto pillow.
- Celebrating: one small "yay" with both arms, then sits back down.

**Best-friend stage detail:** a tiny eucalyptus sprig on the nightcap.

## 5. Pufferfish: "The Puffy Grump"

**Personality:** sweet, but puffs up during PMS: irritable *and* bloated.
Deflated and happy after her period. Grumpy is cute here, never scary.

**Design**

| Element | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Silhouette key | A ball with two tiny side fins and a small tail fin; when puffed, soft rounded bumps all over |
| Body | Normal state: plump rounded lemon shape. Puffed state: near-perfect sphere, about 1.4× volume. Puff level is an animation channel from 0 to 100%. |
| Spikes | Never sharp. Short, rounded nubs like gumdrops that only appear as she puffs. |
| Face | Small pouty "o" mouth, slightly pushed-out cheeks. Eyes get narrower as she puffs. |
| Markings | Pale belly, a scatter of soft round freckle spots on her back |
| Fins | Tiny translucent fins that flutter fast; they look comically small when she is puffed |
| Presentation | She floats inside a soft bubble, so she can share Momo's Room without a tank |
| Moon charm | On a small ribbon bow tied at the base of her top fin |

**Palette:** main aqua mint `#9EDFD6`, secondary cream belly `#FFF6E5`,
accent coral `#FF9E8A` (nubs, bow), freckles `#6FC3BC`.

**Phase poses**

| Phase | Pose |
| --- | --- |
| Menstrual | Puff 40%, slowly sinking and resting on a shell cushion, tired pout, a tiny warm bubble on her belly |
| Follicular | Puff 0%, slim and zippy, big relieved smile, fins fluttering |
| Ovulation | Puff 0%, twirling, bubbles sparkling around her |
| Luteal (PMS) | Puff 100%, round and grumpy, cheeks out, side-eye, fins crossed (as far as they reach) |

**Reactions**

- Gift: stays puffed for one beat, then deflates with a soft "pff" and blushes.
- Happy: a quick loop-the-loop.
- Sad: deflated below normal, drifting downward.
- Sleepy: floats upside down for a second, corrects herself, dozes.
- Celebrating: puffs up on purpose and bounces like a ball among bubble confetti.

**Tone note:** the puffed state is "do not disturb", not rage. No red face, no
angry eyebrows, no sharp spines.

**Best-friend stage detail:** a small pearl on her bow.
